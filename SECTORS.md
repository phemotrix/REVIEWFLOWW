# VORTRIX — 100-Sector Mumbai Pitch Map

**Purpose:** divide Greater Mumbai into 100 contiguous micro-sectors. **One sector = one pitch day** (50–70 pitches, roughly 2–4 km across, bike-walkable).
**Pitch order:** S001 → S100. Tier 1 (premium) first, then Tier 2, then Tier 3.
**Targeting inside every sector:** premium/good businesses (not tiny locals) with **100–1,000 Google reviews** — they feel the pain of review count and can pay ₹2–3k setup.

**Boundaries are working boundaries**, defined by landmarks and roads a salesperson on a bike recognizes. Adjust on the ground; sectors are contiguous by design (no gaps inside Greater Mumbai coverage).

**S001 note:** S001 = Bandra West (Linking Rd / Pali Hill / Carter Rd / Bandstand / Waterfield Rd) — already being researched in parallel. Definition below matches that brief. S005 (Bandra Station West / Hill Rd) is its non-overlapping neighbour to the east.
